# Quick Start

1. Open **VoxRelay**. Complete or skip the optional onboarding walkthrough.
2. Select **Speak**.
3. Choose an **online voice** (English voices are shown by default).
4. Type your phrase in the text box.
5. Press **Enter** or the Speak control to synthesize and play it.
6. To send your synthetic voice to a call or game, enable the **virtual microphone** in VoxRelay, then choose `CABLE Output (VB-Audio Virtual Cable)` as the input device in the other application.
7. Use **Shortcuts** to associate frequently spoken phrases with global hotkeys.

**Tip:** Edge TTS voices require internet. Offline voices require a compatible locally installed engine and model.
