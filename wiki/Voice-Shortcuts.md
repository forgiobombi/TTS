# Voice Shortcuts

Create global shortcuts that speak pre-saved phrases without switching back to the VoxRelay window.

1. Open **Shortcuts**.
2. Enter the phrase.
3. Choose a voice and an available hotkey.
4. Click **Add**.
5. Use the hotkey from another app or game.

Common built-in controls:
- **Ctrl+Shift+F:** focus VoxRelay's text field
- **Ctrl+Shift+V:** toggle compact window
- **Ctrl+Shift+M:** toggle virtual microphone
- **Ctrl+Shift+S:** stop audio
- **Ctrl+Shift+1…9:** quick phrases/shortcuts, depending on configuration

Some hotkeys may conflict with the operating system or another application; choose an unused combination.

Learn more in the app's Shortcut screen. For Discord audio routing see [Virtual Microphone](Virtual-Microphone).
